Audit Playground

You are the auditor. Each company below is a fictional business with a ledger, invoices, payments and exchange rates. Some contain planted accounting errors; some are clean. Find the errors, back each one with evidence, and submit. The same grader that scores AI agents scores you.

  1. Pick a company.
  2. Browse its records. Every page you open costs one step, as it does for an AI agent (100 steps maximum).
  3. For each error: choose its type, the affected invoice or payment, the amount, and the records that prove it.
  4. Submit. You see your score, the answer key, and how the baseline policies did on the same company.
