Play the Auditor

Each company is fictional. Some books hide planted errors; some are clean. Find the errors, prove them with records, and the same grader that scores AI agents scores you.

  1. Choose a company from the ledger.
  2. Open pages of its records. Each page costs one step of 100, exactly as it does for an agent.
  3. Draft a finding: type, affected document, amount and the records that prove it.
  4. Submit, then compare yourself with the baseline policies and read the answer key.
